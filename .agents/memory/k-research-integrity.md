---
name: K research integrity
description: Limits on interpreting K evidence and reusing historical holdouts.
---

The existing SOXL research history has already been examined in other studies.
A new K-specific chronological split is not a genuinely untouched holdout.
Do not promote K from an unvalidated screening heuristic based on this reused sample.

**Why:** Other experiments already exposed these market outcomes, and single-symbol
temporal ranks cannot establish cross-sectional investment-selection ability.

**How to apply:** Keep failure reports and sparse or empty tied ranks visible.
Do not change frozen rank boundaries after reading outcomes to make an empty
comparison usable. A new protocol must distinguish exploratory development
from evaluation on genuinely unseen outcomes.
