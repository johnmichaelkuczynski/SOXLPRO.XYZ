SOXL Analysis Methodology

This page defines the evidence behind the interactive SOXL research tool: historical probabilities, benchmark comparisons, options diagnostics, volatility, dislocation, and backtesting.

Historical probability

Probability results count historical rolling windows in which SOXL reached a requested price-move magnitude within a selected trading horizon. The result is a historical frequency, not an implied forecast. The tool displays the number of analogues so a small sample is visible rather than hidden.

Benchmarks and dislocation

Benchmark-based analysis compares SOXL with assets such as QQQ, TQQQ, TLT, XLU, and VIX. The SOXL-QQQ dislocation view uses normalized relative performance and a rolling baseline to identify periods that may merit closer inspection. Correlation and historical similarity are not causation.

Options risk and volatility surface

The options views are descriptive. Call risk/reward uses option premium and modeled outcomes; the volatility-surface view highlights discrepancies in the available SOXL chain and labels directional BUY/SELL pressure. Quotes, liquidity, spreads, and implied volatility can change quickly.

Call-sleeve backtest

The default allocation engine models a 20% call sleeve and 80% cash. Long SOXL calls are priced with Black-Scholes using trailing realized volatility, with a default 45-day maturity and rolls near 10 days. Deviation from QQQ influences sleeve sizing. The engine reports return, CAGR, volatility, drawdown, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, capital at risk, and return per unit of at-risk capital.

Signals are lagged before being applied to returns to avoid look-ahead leakage. A model backtest is a historical simulation and does not include every live-trading friction or future regime.

Limitations

SOXL is a daily 3× leveraged ETF and can experience rapid, substantial losses. Nothing on this site is financial advice or a guarantee of future performance. Validate assumptions, data dates, liquidity, taxes, and your own risk capacity independently.
