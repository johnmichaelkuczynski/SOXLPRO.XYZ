CONTEXT: The app's daily SOXL signal is built on a composite historical-range percentile. Backtests (2010-03-11 to 2026-10-20) show it works at long horizons but not short ones. At 1 year: BUY 93.2% finished higher vs 67.6% baseline; POSSIBLE BUY 83.2%; DO NOTHING 69.6%; POSSIBLE SELL 53.7%, so the ordering is clean. At 1 month, POSSIBLE BUY finished higher 53.0% vs a 59.6% baseline, worse than buying on any day. Paying users mostly trade 1-month horizons and need a buy signal that clearly beats baseline over that horizon. The fix has two parts.

PART 1 - LONG-TERM SIGNAL (keep and tighten):
1. Keep the existing composite historical-range percentile logic unchanged, but relabel it "Long-term signal (6-12 months)".
2. Tighten its buy thresholds: BUY <= 5% percentile; POSSIBLE BUY > 5% and <= 15%; DO NOTHING from > 15% up to the current POSSIBLE SELL lower bound. Leave sell-side thresholds unchanged.
3. Put all thresholds in one config object (e.g., RATING_THRESHOLDS); no hardcoded values elsewhere. Generate the signal bar, band labels and explanatory text from the config.

PART 2 - NEW SHORT-TERM SIGNAL (1 month / 21 trading days):
1. Build a separate "Short-term signal (1 month)" displayed alongside the long-term one, with ratings BUY / POSSIBLE BUY / NO EDGE (no sell side needed for now).
2. Candidate inputs, computed using only data available on each date (no lookahead):
   - % deviation of close from its 20-day and 50-day moving averages
   - 5-day and 10-day returns
   - RSI(14)
   - drawdown from the 20-day high
   - a confirmation trigger to avoid buying into an ongoing collapse: e.g., close above the prior day's high, or close back above the 5-day moving average after being below it
   - a regime filter: whether SOXL (or the SOX index if available) is above or below its 200-day moving average
   - the long-term percentile from Part 1, as one input
3. Build a small number of simple, interpretable rules from these (thresholds and AND-combinations; no machine-learning models, no more than 3 conditions per rule).
4. WALK-FORWARD VALIDATION IS MANDATORY: choose rules and thresholds using 2010-03-11 to 2018-12-31 only, freeze them, then evaluate on 2019-01-01 to 2026-10-20. Report in-sample and out-of-sample results separately.
5. Acceptance criteria, measured OUT-OF-SAMPLE with non-overlapping 21-day holding periods: the short-term BUY must beat the all-day 1-month "finished higher" baseline by at least 10 percentage points, with at least 30 non-overlapping observations. If no rule meets this, the short-term signal must display "NO EDGE" every day; do not ship a rule that fails the criteria.
6. When the short-term signal fires, also display that rule's historical out-of-sample win rate, median return, and average worst dip below entry.
